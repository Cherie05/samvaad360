## Prototype/MVP brief

Samvaad 360 is a Snowflake-backed lender workbench joining loan and repayment facts with emails, chats and call transcripts. Five public workspaces provide a priority queue, Customer 360, cited evidence, review simulation and an interactive Call studio. Users compare new customer context against policy, then rehearse permission, fictional account-holder confirmation, borrower replies, opt-out and human handoff. New hardship evidence stops an earlier growth invitation and shows the revised decision with its source. Optional browser speech controls accompany the text conversation; real calls and financial changes are disabled. The live website reads 20 fictional customers from Snowflake and passed 11 hosted browser checks. Public reviews and conversations belong only to that visit; private demo reviews persist separately in Snowflake. Production identities, SIP delivery and actual CoCo execution remain pending.
