## Prototype/MVP brief

Samvaad 360 unifies structured loan and repayment data with emails, chats and call transcripts in Snowflake. Support teams see an evidence-backed customer journey and recommendations for hardship support, retention or a conditional top-up, with consent and DND checks. The public website reads 20 fictional customers from Snowflake and demonstrates customer queries, cited answers, a review simulation and conversation handoff. A private operator app saves demo reviews in Snowflake; the local voice lab supports speech and persisted conversations. Public review decisions are visit-local simulations; real financial changes and telephone calls are disabled.
